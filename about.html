Professional Summary

Gautam Gohel is a Cloud Operations and System Administration professional with more than 11 years of experience in IT infrastructure management, cloud operations, observability, and technical support. He specializes in maintaining reliable and high-availability production environments across multi-cloud platforms. He has hands-on experience with major cloud technologies including Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Gautam is highly skilled in monitoring and observability tools such as Datadog,AWS CloudWatch, Grafana, Sentry, Azure App Insights, and Uptrends. His expertise includes incident response, troubleshooting, system monitoring, server maintenance, and cloud optimization. He has successfully reduced cloud infrastructure costs through rightsizing and optimization initiatives while improving application performance and operational efficiency. He also automated operational runbooks and repetitive tasks, helping engineering teams save time and focus on high-priority incidents. In addition to cloud operations, Gautam has strong experience in Windows and Linux administration, Office 365, Active Directory, firewall management, endpoint security, and network troubleshooting. He is experienced in working within high-availability, on-call, and SLA-driven environments where reliability and rapid incident resolution are critical. Gautam holds certifications in cloud and infrastructure technologies, including Google Cloud Associate Cloud Engineer, Microsoft Azure Administrator (AZ-104), AWS cloud certifications, Fortinet NSE certifications, and cybersecurity training programs. His passion for cloud technologies, automation, observability, and reliability engineering continues to drive his professional growth and contribution to modern IT operations environments.
